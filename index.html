WHO IT’S FOR

Athletes and operators who want systems, not hype.

Best fit: athletes, teams, and high-output operators whose bodies are already under load and who need training that respects structure and recovery—basketball, track, field sports, and movement-heavy work.

It is for people who want to understand how their body works and are willing to train accordingly. It is not a fit for shortcuts, novelty drills, or quick-fix promises. The work is grounded, measured, and built to hold up under real seasons and stress.

HOW IT WORKS

Structure first. Load next. Then performance.

Principle
Tensegrity as the organizing idea.

Training respects the body’s tensegrity—compression and tension balanced across structures—instead of isolating parts that never work alone.

Principle
Anatomy Trains as the map.

Lines of pull and fascia trains guide programming. Drills and loads follow the map of how the body actually moves, not just how it looks.

Principle
Repeatable systems, not one-off workouts.

Programs are built as systems you can run—clear progressions, rest structures, and checkpoints—so you can sustain performance across seasons.

Principle
Recovery is part of the system.

Recovery protocols are designed with the same care as training sessions. Health herbs-only work connects directly to how you train.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is this for pros only?

No. The methodology is built for serious athletes and movers at any level who are willing to train with structure and discipline.

Do you provide medical advice?

No. Texas Movement Performance designs training systems and recovery protocols. Health herbs-only work and medical care are separate. Always work with qualified medical professionals.

Can you work with my team remotely?

In many cases, yes. Team systems can be designed with remote assessment and teaching, then refined in person as needed.

How should I start?

Start with a short note about your current training, injuries or history, and what you need your body to do over the next season. We scope from there.
