The TMP Methodology

Two frameworks. One complete system.

Most training fails at the plateau because it treats the body as isolated parts. TMP treats it as a continuous tension network — and programs accordingly.

Tensegrity Engineering
Applies structural engineering principles to the human body. Reveals how force is distributed, stored, and transferred across the whole system via continuous tension networks.
  • Structural stability through tension
  • Force distribution optimization
  • Movement efficiency principles
Tom Myers — Anatomy Trains
Myofascial meridian mapping identifying continuous fascial lines connecting the entire body. Reveals where movement patterns break down and compensatory chains originate.
  • Myofascial continuity mapping
  • Movement pattern integration
  • Compensatory chain identification
Full Spectrum Result
Where the two systems converge
Tensegrity structural principles + Anatomy Trains fascial mapping = a diagnostic framework that identifies and eliminates performance plateaus across all movement dimensions.
