TRAINING · MOVEMENT · RECOVERY

Train like it’s engineering.

Training systems, movement education, and recovery protocols built on repeatability, discipline, and sustainable output—not motivation and random intensity.

Texas Movement Performance is the physical layer of the ecosystem. It is for athletes, coaches, and high-output operators who treat training as engineering. It solves the problem of effort that does not compound because it is not structured. It works by prioritizing structure over intensity, running repeatable protocols, programming recovery on purpose, and adjusting from what you measure. It connects to Health for herbs-only recovery support, HERO for footwear that reflects how the foot actually moves, and Media for documentation. The body responds to structure. So we program structure and recover on purpose.

What this page is

This is the physical systems layer—training, movement, and recovery—plus direct access to the long-form course and YouTube content that teach the methodology end to end.

Who it’s for

Athletes and coaches who want programming and movement education they can apply, teach, and repeat, not just a pile of workouts.

Operators with real output demands who need training and recovery that hold up over months, not just for a single phase.

Anyone who wants to understand why a protocol works—not just follow it. It is not for people chasing intensity for its own sake. Sustainable output is the goal.

How it works

Structure over intensity. We build systems and progressions first; intensity is programmed inside them, not the other way around.

Repeatable protocols. Training is designed to be repeatable and measurable so the body can adapt instead of guess.

Recover on purpose. Recovery—sleep, load management, and routine—is treated as part of the work, not an afterthought.

Measure and adjust. We track signals that matter and adjust from data, not vibes.

Frequently asked questions

What is Texas Movement Performance?

It is the physical layer of Texas Movement—training systems, movement education, and recovery protocols built on a systems-first view of the body.

Who is it for?

Athletes, coaches, and high-output operators who want repeatable, sustainable output rather than random intensity.

Is this personal training?

It is systems and programming. Individual coaching may be part of it, but the core product is a repeatable structure you can run and teach.

How does recovery fit in?

Recovery is programmed, not optional. Health provides herbs-only recovery support where it fits inside the larger system.

How does this connect to the rest of Texas Movement?

It shares recovery with Health, footwear with HERO, and documentation with Media, all under the company layer at Texas Movement International.

Where do I learn the full methodology?

Through the Elite Secrets course on Udemy and the long-form videos on the Texas Movement Performance YouTube channel.
