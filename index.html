SYSTEMS & METHODS

Training built on tensegrity, anatomy trains, and recovery discipline.

Layer
Movement assessment.

We start by seeing how you move under load—where tension sits, where structures are overworking, and how that shows up in performance and fatigue.

Layer
Systemized programming.

Training is built in blocks with clear intent, volume, and progression. Sessions serve the block; the block serves the season; the season serves your long-term movement.

Layer
Recovery protocols.

Recovery is structured, not improvised—routines and herbs-only protocols that respect load, sleep, and nervous system recovery, connected to Health by Texas Movement.

Layer
Documentation.

We document the system—what we’re doing, why, and how it changes—so you and your coach have a record you can reference and refine.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is this just another workout program?

No. It is a systems-first approach to training and recovery with assessment, programming, and documentation. Work is designed to compound over time.

Who is it a fit for?

Athletes, coaches, and serious movers who care about structure and are willing to train with discipline. Casual fitness or purely aesthetic goals are a poor fit.

Is this medical advice?

No. Training and recovery work here are educational and systems-focused. They do not replace medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice.

Do I need Elite Secrets to work with TMP?

No, but it helps. The course teaches the underlying methodology so coaching and team work can move faster and go deeper.

Where can I see more of the work?
